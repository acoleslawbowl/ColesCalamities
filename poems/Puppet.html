Puppet

Cole Stanton

July 25th, 2026

There are strings
On your limbs
They don’t push
They only pull
And let go

Green yarn
Red yarn
A million others
They twist, turn
In a haze of innocence
It meanders
Like a crowd
Interweaving
None alike
Your eyes
Are overwhelmed

Well—
Then are the fingers
Which guide your little wooden limbs
Smell of someone who just ate?
Or of iron and earth
Whose hands are bloody and grimy
Maybe both
Only then
Do you realize
Red and green fuzz are falling
Not from the strings
But from your nose

They drift down
Swaying into your hands
If you were not looking
You would not have felt it
The wind bristles your clothes
But your hands are still held out

Someone from somewhere
Laughs at you
They say something
Dismissive, mocking
But your ears weren’t ever keen
Your primitive brain
Latches onto rhythm, melody
Suddenly
It’s as if you are free

The fuzz in the air
Tastes no different
Than the grime of it
It’s not like
You ever noticed it,
So focused on the crowd

You laugh
At the pigeon with eyes too wide
Who nibbles on a crumb of rye
It is stupid and unknowning
So we insist
With twisted superiority
We must be different
