I drew a picture of The Weather

Cole Stanton

November 9th, 2026

What a wonder it is
Your erratic white glow pierces the sky in jagged streaks
Crumbling it all into paper and of charcoal edges
For only a snapshot and gone in a sweep
Stretching through, yearning downstairs
You meet your match
You proclaim your peace, you victor
As the crystals in your delicately dangerous glass crack
You become more attenuated than the tiny audience who watched your last track

Your life was spectacular
But you frowned as you shattered
Fractions of a milliseconds spent yearning Searching for your beloved coupling
Rewarded with two thousand milliseconds of bliss, rapture and benediction
Your lips weren’t coaxed downwards by disappointment but by pity
You perform this show on the city-stage
And not one from your admiring audience will ever be afforded your same simplicity
You gaze down upon them in perplexity
Wondering how they come to live with such complexity

Some unimportant couple takes offense
Ever caught up in the deepness of their affection
When they profess their love to another,
You become hardly more than a peculiar observer,
“It’s all gonna end someday
But we’re still here if tomorrow rains
‘Cause today’s today and tomorrow stupid
And I’ll still love you in the morning”
They hardly even recognized you
And yet, suddenly, blinded by the light of their brilliance
Immediately vulnerable to their unbelievably carefree existential cadence
They both affirmed your inevitable demise and
Criticized your short life

As you roll across the clouds
Like white watercolor spilling across a swirling dark canvas
Once more you gaze down your jagged edges
You proclaim yourself unlovable
You say you can’t love like that and therefore
How could they reciprocate?
You say, so maybe it is, maybe that’s how it is
Your gaze offhandedly transfixed,
Meeting a pair of dark eyes
A boy under the canopy,
Undeniably reverent, full of awe, his face
And yet, you face yourself heedlessly
Prideful with denial and deceit

Your love story was only ever a sham
You exclaimed that he was your man
You fantasized that she was holding your hand
You looked down upon her eyes from the sky
Puddles, lakes, oceans reflecting the starry night up so high
Like a Tristan chord, you felt utterly unlike how you identify
You loveless beast, your unsymmetrical eye
And your crookedly dangerous lines
Carelessly, you spell out your soliloquy
"I wish I didn't look"
"Into your pretty eyes"
You boast, say that you took the stage light
When almost everybody forgot you the following night
You looked at that couple and their two pairs of loving sight
Captured your connection and showed them with pride
As if someone of her beauty, timelessness, excellence
Would consider your gross affection
And the result is dejection
So you strike the pole
And not the field
God forbid you set her skin on fire
You forgettable liar
