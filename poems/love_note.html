love note

Cole Stanton

September 27th, 2026

You always think you are better
Your hair luscious
Your lashes long
And then people pass you
And with ease you could find a hundred compliments
Their shade of hair is a special auburn with a healthy gleam under the sun
Another’s lashes are shorter but god are their eyes are worthy of love
And you never stood a chance to stand out

‘If you
Looked right into my eyes
You’ll see some weird reflection
Of someone’s pretty smile’
It submerges you
And in the slime it lulls you
It tells you the fantasy of another love story
And it couldn’t ever be real
When you never stood close enough -
It’s easy to forget, anyway,
That your purple is my green
That our perception is never ‘clean’
The world is filled with intricacies
So you never saw how my eyes reflected the traffic light sheen

Do you blame it on your looks?
As if by looking in the mirror long enough
You’ll find some minuscule evidence
But they still blush
When you blink
Before you realize it
You’ve stared so long and forward
That ridiculed reflection is now adored

Your first instinct
Is to banish that voice which tells you
That you might not be the admired the way you admire
But rather, you embrace it
Whatever was the reinforcement to that golden rule
Left the support beams open to crumble
Surrounded by people who either
Look into your dark eyes face value
Or gaze into the scripture etched into your skin
It foretells that your success is uncertain
And may never be determined
They were the acid
They see nothing else
Another face in the crowd
Always one or the other
And never in between

So why should you even try?
You’re told that
You should go and talk to the other side
That happiness and solitude cannot coincide
As if watering the plants of your friendships
Took that precious ‘i’ from the former
And magnified the latter to look timeless
Blameless at most can you place them
Their own stories have rewritten what constitutes happiness
So when they look through the ink of their own writing
And see you with white space on both sides
You realize,
It’s not so easy to look in between the lines

Doubtless, it is still you who wonders
Why is it always my side,
That is supposed to prove itself?
People will gaze at a word
Starting with ‘L’ ending with ‘E’
With an ‘O’ and ‘V’ in between
They’ll take these letters, squeeze them
Add new ones, stretch those
And they’ll arrive at some abstract word
Their prose declared you a saint
But their definition of religion
Was only ever based in egocentrism
It placed a chokehold on what was supposed to be a single standard
And they became the proud privileged of the double standard
The original love was meant to be shared, generously given
Their insistence that it must be earned in a sick one sided fashion
That it must only be possible on some certain benign conditions
Is what brought the death of it

And yet
Here you stand,
Son to a party girl
And daughter of a businessman
In constant bitter competition
Their worth to another
Written at the bottom of the paycheck
You stand under that semi-translucent white sheet
You stare up at it, with wide eyes, petrified,
That same prophecy will slither its way into your life unconsciously
For, you are the product of an antithesis
Of the very ‘inauthenticity’ you swear you stood against

This is the world that you profess your unbridled love to
Filled with impossible allure and unimaginably complex tragedies and idiosyncrasies
You look at it with reverent eyes, in awe of its countless beauties
All for the similarity,
You impatiently wait for your effortless but not unhonored departure from the face of it
You hold this to be self-evident
